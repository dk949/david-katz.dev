Research

Focus

My research sits at the intersection of compiler construction, high-performance computing, and domain-specific languages. I work primarily with LLVM and its Multi-Level Intermediate Representation (MLIR) framework, developing compiler passes and dialect extensions for heterogeneous compute targets.

The core challenge I work on is bridging the gap between high-level algorithmic descriptions and the deeply heterogeneous hardware landscape — CPUs, GPUs, FPGAs, and emerging accelerators — by encoding domain knowledge into the compiler rather than relying solely on target-independent optimisations.

Interests

  • MLIR dialect design and progressive lowering strategies
  • Polyhedral optimisation and affine loop transformations
  • Auto-vectorisation and SIMD code generation
  • Compiler support for parallel and distributed memory models
  • Performance portability across heterogeneous architectures
  • Systems programming languages and language runtimes
