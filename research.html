Research

Focus

My research primarily focuses on utilising the Cerebras WSE architecture for HPC tasks, like fluid dynamics or weather simulations. I am using the xDSL framework to build compiler infrastructure to target this system. Presently my research is focused on transforming high level Fortran code, annotated with OpenMP directives, into highly optimised CSL programs.

The core challenge in my work is mapping the OpenMP shared memory programming model onto the WSE's highly parallel distributed memory hardware.

Interests

  • MLIR dialect design and progressive lowering strategies
  • Systems programming languages and language runtimes
  • Novel architectures
