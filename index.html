About

I'm a PhD student in High Performance Computing at EPCC, The University of Edinburgh, specialising in compiler design for novel architectures. My work focuses on the LLVM and MLIR framework, building dialects and transformations that target the Cerebras WSE architecture for HPC tasks.

My main interests are in compilers, programming languages, systems software, and anything and everything low level. Post-graduation I'm looking for roles in compiler engineering, HPC toolchain development, or systems programming.
