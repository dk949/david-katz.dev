About

I'm a doctoral researcher in High Performance Computing, specialising in compiler design and optimisation. My work focuses on LLVM and its Multi-Level Intermediate Representation (MLIR) framework — building dialect extensions and transformation passes that target the performance challenges of heterogeneous and parallel computing architectures.

I'm broadly interested in compilers, language runtimes, and the systems software that bridges algorithms and hardware. Post-graduation I'm looking for roles in compiler engineering, HPC toolchain development, or systems programming.
